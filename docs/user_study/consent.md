# Participation information and consent

**Study.** StepLens user study, part of a DS-GA 3001 course project (NYU, Fall 2026).

**What you will do.** You will use two interfaces, for 15 minutes each, to find where a
video model makes mistakes, and fill in a short questionnaire after each. The session
takes about 50 minutes. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

**What we record.** The findings you write down, the time at which you write them, your
questionnaire answers, and written notes from a short conversation at the end. We do not
record audio or video of you. Your name is not stored with the data; you get a
participant number.

**Use of the data.** Results are reported in aggregate in the course report and
presentation. Individual answer sheets are kept only by the project team and deleted at
the end of the course.

**Risks and benefits.** No risks beyond those of ordinary computer use. No compensation.
