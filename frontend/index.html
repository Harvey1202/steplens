Miss rate by error category × model

Cell = share of that category's error steps the model misses at the current threshold. Last row = false-alarm rate on normal steps. Click a cell to show only that category in the other views (click again to clear); click a column title to make that model A. The model makes one error/normal decision per step, so a step with several error categories counts in every row it belongs to and is caught or missed in all of them at once; rows overlap and do not add up to the number of error steps.

Slices with the most missed errors

Metadata subgroups (recipe, person, kitchen, step duration, position in recipe, optionally × category), ranked by miss rate above the model's overall rate. The seeds and models columns show whether other training runs and other models flag the same slice. Click a row to apply it as a filter.

Recipes by miss rate

Pick a recipe to see its task graph with each step colored by how often the model misses errors there.

Recording timeline

Ground-truth steps (colored by error category) above the models' per-second error scores. Click a step to inspect it and jump the video; drag across the scores to scrub. While the video plays, a line marks its time and the details follow the step on screen.

Step features (UMAP)

Each dot is one step, placed by UMAP from the mean of its frozen segment features, which is what the error-recognition heads see. Steps outside the current phase and filters are drawn faint. If dots group by person or recording rather than by outcome, the features say more about who recorded a step than about whether it went wrong; the table below measures this in the full feature space.
